My story

I'm Abdullah Al Mamun, an Administrative Coordinator based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I lead day-to-day operations for Gcontent, a media organization of more than 50 people working across editorial, marketing, design, and administration.

I didn't start in operations. I joined Gcontent in January 2022 as a Junior Graphic Designer, making visual and video content in Illustrator, Photoshop, Premiere Pro, and Canva. Within a year I moved into social media management, where I planned a data-driven strategy that grew audience engagement by 30% and learned how to keep designers, editors, and writers delivering on time.

In 2024 I moved into HR & Admin, looking after documentation, attendance, leave records, and recruitment from job posting through onboarding. In April 2025 I was promoted to Administrative Coordinator, my third promotion in three years, and took on responsibility for daily operations, policy, and monthly reporting to leadership.

Along the way I noticed how much of my own job was repetitive: compiling reports, assigning tasks, chasing updates, emailing candidates. So I started automating it. What began as a few time-savers grew into a toolkit of 11 automations now running in production, built mainly with n8n, Google Workspace, and Zoho, and together saving the team more than 30 hours every week.

Today my work sits where people and systems meet: keeping a growing team organised, and building the automations that let them spend their time on work that actually needs a person.
